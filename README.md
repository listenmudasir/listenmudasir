## 🧪 Current Research

**Intelligent Multimedia Systems Lab (IMSLab), NTPU**  
*Research Assistant | 2025 – Present*

- **Illegal Waste Dumping Detection & Start-Time Localization**  
  Developed an **onset-centric temporal detection framework** using VideoMAE-based spatiotemporal encoders, motion/environmental cues, and multi-view temporal voting for accurate boundary localization in long surveillance videos.

- **Pedestrian Attribute Recognition (PAR 2025 Challenge)**  
  Proposed a **region-aware multimodal framework** combining **CLIP embeddings and Graph Neural Networks (GNNs)** for structured attribute reasoning.  
  → Published as a **full paper at CAIP 2025 (Springer LNCS)**.

- **Gaze Estimation for Under-Display Camera (UDC) Images**  
  Built an AI-based pipeline integrating **SCUNet**, **MobileViT**, and geometric modeling for robust gaze estimation in degraded imaging conditions.

- **Multi-Camera People Tracking (AI City Challenge 2024)**  
  Designed a system with **tracklet refinement, appearance modeling, and spatio-temporal clustering** to reduce ID switches and improve cross-camera association.
